Bereavement
To: ANZ Bank New Zealand
Re: Estate of Robert James Whitford
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to notify ANZ Bank New Zealand of the death of my father, Robert James Whitford, who passed away on [date of death]. I am his son and the executor named in his will, and I am writing in that capacity as I begin to administer his estate.

I do not have a specific account number to hand, and would be grateful if you could locate any accounts held in his name from the details provided.
…plus the documents they'll ask for, and a line to stop all mail in his name — ready to sign and send.
Diagnosis
Partners Life · Income Protection · Call Script
What to say
Hi, my name is [your full name] and I hold an income protection policy with Partners Life. I'd like to speak with someone who can help me understand whether I can make a claim under my policy, and if so, how to go about it.
What to ask
— Does this policy pay out on diagnosis alone, or only once I am unable to work?
— Is there a stand-down or waiting period before payments begin, and how long is the benefit period?
…plus the stand-downs and exclusions to listen for — the small print that decides whether money actually arrives.
Incapacity
To Liam, Gemma, and the family group
Re Dad — and what we need to do differently
Hi everyone,

I want to let you all know that Dad's dementia has progressed quite a bit over the last few months. He's had a couple of incidents at home that reminded me he really can't manage safely on his own anymore.

What would make the biggest difference right now is regular phone or video calls with Dad — even just a quick check-in a few times a week.
…plus the specific, low-pressure ways each of them can help from a distance — turning "let me know if you need anything" into something real.
